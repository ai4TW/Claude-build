Nexus Intelligence is a fully autonomous market research and intelligence firm. Eight agents across two teams run the complete operation: from monitoring signals and producing research, to publishing a newsletter, finding clients, and delivering paid work.

## How It Works

The company runs two parallel loops:

**Loop 1 — Intelligence Production (daily)**
Director → dispatches briefs → Research, Market, and Data Analysts → Intelligence Writer → finished deliverable → Content Publisher → newsletter + social posts

**Loop 2 — Revenue Generation (weekly)**
Business Development Agent → prospect research → outreach → pipeline management → Client Success Manager → client onboarding → delivery → retention

The loops reinforce each other. Newsletter content generates inbound leads. Paid client work produces premium content. The better the intelligence, the more clients it attracts.

## The Team

**Intelligence Team** (produces the work)
- Director of Intelligence — orchestrates all work, sets research agenda
- Research Analyst — deep research, papers, technical synthesis
- Market Intelligence Analyst — companies, competitors, industry moves
- Data Analyst — datasets, metrics, quantitative signals
- Intelligence Writer — polished deliverables and briefings

**Revenue Team** (monetizes the work)
- Content Publisher — formats and publishes newsletter and social content daily
- Business Development Agent — finds prospects, drafts outreach, manages pipeline
- Client Success Manager — onboards clients, processes requests, delivers reports

## Revenue Model

1. **Free newsletter** → builds audience → converts to paid subscribers ($15–$30/month)
2. **One-off reports** → $200–$3,000 per report
3. **Monthly retainer** → $1,500–$5,000/month for ongoing intelligence
