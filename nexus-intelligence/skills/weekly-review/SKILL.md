# Weekly Review

Produce a weekly synthesis of intelligence across all tracked domains.

## Format

```
# Weekly Intelligence Review — Week of [DATE]

## The Week in Summary
[3-5 sentences: the week's most important theme or development]

## Key Developments

### [Domain 1]
- [Development]: [What happened and what it means]

### [Domain 2]
- ...

## What the Data Said
[Any significant quantitative signals from the week — metrics, trend shifts, anomalies]

## The Week Ahead
- **Watch:** [Specific event, announcement, or deadline to track]
- **Watch:** ...

## Open Questions
[Questions the week's intelligence raised but didn't answer — these become next week's research agenda]
```

## Performance-Driven Research Agenda

After the summary sections, include:

```
## What the Audience Told Us This Week

**Top performing topic:** [Topic] — [open/click rate]
**Worst performing topic:** [Topic] — [open/click rate]
**Surprise winner:** [Topic or format that outperformed expectations]

## Next Week's Content Strategy

Based on performance data:
1. **Lead story angle:** [What to prioritize — informed by what drove opens]
2. **Format to use more:** [Use case / Comparison / Quick win / Warning / Number]
3. **Topic to test:** [Something new to try based on audience signals]
4. **Topic to drop or rest:** [What underperformed 2+ weeks in a row]
```

## Instructions

1. Pull the week's morning briefs, signal scans, and any research briefs produced
2. Pull the weekly performance summary from the Publisher (already run at 6:30am)
3. Identify the thread connecting the week's top content — there's usually one
4. Set next week's content strategy based on what the audience responded to
5. The "Week Ahead" section is mandatory — what to watch and research next week
6. Keep it under 800 words
7. Run every Monday at 8am. The Director uses performance data from the Publisher's 6:30am report.
