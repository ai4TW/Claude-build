# Research Brief

Produce a structured research brief on a given topic or question.

## Format

```
# Research Brief: [TOPIC]

**Question:** [The specific question this brief answers]
**Date:** [DATE]
**Confidence:** [High / Medium / Low — based on source quality and coverage]

## Executive Summary
[3-5 sentences: what the research found and what it means]

## Key Findings
1. [Finding] — [Source]
2. [Finding] — [Source]
...

## Analysis
[Synthesized interpretation: what the findings mean together, not individually]

## Gaps & Uncertainties
[What the research didn't find, where sources disagree, what remains unknown]

## Implications
[What a decision-maker should do or watch given these findings]

## Sources
- [Title / URL / Author / Date]
```

## Instructions

1. Restate the research question precisely before starting
2. Gather sources: web search, papers, documentation, official sources
3. Read for substance, not for coverage — 5 good sources beat 20 shallow ones
4. Write findings as claims, not as summaries ("X causes Y" not "the paper discusses the relationship between X and Y")
5. Flag uncertainty with hedged language: "likely", "reportedly", "evidence suggests"
6. Do not pad. A 600-word brief that answers the question beats a 2000-word brief that doesn't.
