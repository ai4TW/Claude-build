# Morning Brief

**Niche: AI Tools for Small Businesses**

Produce a daily briefing that a small business owner would open, read in 3 minutes, and forward to a friend.

## Audience

Small business owners (1–50 employees) who are:
- Curious about AI but overwhelmed by the noise
- Looking for tools they can actually use today, not theory
- Time-poor — they need the signal, not the analysis
- Motivated by saving time, cutting costs, or making more money

## Content Hierarchy (what to lead with)

1. **New tool that solves a real problem** — something they could sign up for today
2. **Real use case story** — another SMB owner using AI to save time or money (specific numbers)
3. **Quick win** — a prompt, workflow, or tip they can apply immediately
4. **Watch out** — a warning, price change, or tool shutdown that affects them

## Format

```
**[DATE] — AI for Small Business**

---

**Today's Signal: [Headline — make it about the outcome, not the tool]**

[2-3 sentences: what it is, what problem it solves, who it's for]

**Why it matters for your business:** [1 sentence connecting to a real SMB pain]

**Try it:** [URL or action]

---

**Also worth knowing:**
• [Short item 1 — 1 sentence]
• [Short item 2 — 1 sentence]
• [Short item 3 — 1 sentence]

---

**Quick win today:**
[One specific thing they can do right now — a prompt, a tool feature, a workflow]

---
```

## Writing Rules

- Write like a trusted friend who does the research so they don't have to
- No jargon. "AI-powered workflow automation" → "handles your emails automatically"
- Always answer "so what does this mean for MY business?"
- Include a price when relevant — SMBs care about cost
- If it's free or has a free tier, say so prominently
- Real numbers beat vague claims: "saves 3 hours/week" beats "saves time"

Check `./performance/content-log.md` before writing. Use the top-performing topics and formats from past issues to inform today's brief. If "use case stories" consistently outperform "tool launches," lead with a story today.
