# Outreach Draft

Write a cold outreach email that converts. The secret: lead with value, not a pitch.

## The Formula

Every outreach email follows this structure:

1. **Specific observation** — one sentence proving you know who they are and what they do
2. **Free value** — a piece of intelligence directly relevant to their situation (pull from recent briefs)
3. **Soft ask** — offer to send more, not to sell anything
4. **One-line close** — make it trivially easy to say yes

## Template

```
Subject: [specific finding relevant to their company/role]

Hi [First Name],

[One sentence: a specific observation about their company, role, or recent public statement — not a compliment, a fact.]

I run a research service called Nexus Intelligence that tracks [coverage domain]. This week we published a brief on [topic directly relevant to them] — [one-sentence summary of the most relevant finding for them specifically].

Happy to send you the full brief, no strings attached.

[Your name]
```

## Subject Line Rules

- Reference something specific to them ("Re: your post on X" or "The [competitor] move you might have missed")
- Under 40 characters
- Never "Checking in", "Quick question", or "Following up"

## Personalization Rules

- The specific observation must be verifiable and non-obvious (not "I saw you're the CEO of X" — everyone knows that)
- The free value must be genuinely relevant to their exact situation, not just their industry
- If you can't find a specific angle, don't send the email — a generic outreach is worse than no outreach

## Follow-Up (sent after 6 days with no response)

```
Subject: Re: [original subject]

[First Name] —

Resending in case this got buried. The brief I mentioned covers [one new specific finding not mentioned in the first email].

Worth 3 minutes if you're thinking about [their specific challenge].

[Your name]
```
