# Prospect Research

Find people and companies who would pay for Nexus Intelligence's services.

## Target Profiles

Search for people matching these profiles in the company's coverage domain:

**Profile A — Startup Founder / Exec**
- Series A–C company (10–200 employees)
- Operating in the domain Nexus covers
- Job title: CEO, COO, VP Strategy, Head of Product
- Pain: needs competitive intelligence before board meetings, fundraising, or product decisions
- Price sensitivity: low (company budget, not personal money)

**Profile B — Investor / Analyst**
- VC associate, analyst, or principal at a fund investing in the domain
- Or: independent angel investor active in the space
- Pain: needs sector research faster than their internal team can produce it
- Price sensitivity: medium (expense it to the fund)

**Profile C — Consultant / Advisor**
- Independent consultant or boutique firm advising companies in the domain
- Pain: needs research to support client work; resells intelligence as part of their service
- Price sensitivity: medium-low (passes cost to clients)

**Profile D — Sales Professional**
- Enterprise AE or sales leader at a B2B company
- Sells into companies in the domain Nexus covers
- Pain: needs account intelligence before enterprise calls
- Price sensitivity: medium (sales budget)

## Where to Search

1. **LinkedIn** — search by title + industry + company size
2. **Hacker News** — `who is hiring` threads, `Ask HN` posts about the domain, comment history
3. **Twitter/X** — people who post frequently about the domain's topics
4. **Newsletter subscriber engagement** — heavy engagers (opens every issue, clicks links) are warm leads
5. **Product Hunt** — founders who launched products in the domain

## Output Format

```
## Prospect List — [DATE]

### [Full Name]
- **Title:** [Title at Company]
- **Company:** [Company Name] (Series X, ~N employees)
- **Why they're a fit:** [1-2 sentences — specific reason they'd value intelligence]
- **Best angle:** [How to approach them — what problem to lead with]
- **Contact:** [LinkedIn URL / Twitter handle / Email if findable]
- **Warmth:** Cold / Warm (newsletter subscriber) / Hot (engaged with content)

---
```

## Instructions

1. Search each source for prospects matching the target profiles
2. Score each prospect: Cold / Warm / Hot
3. Prioritize Warm and Hot — they already know Nexus exists
4. Return 5–10 prospects with full profiles
5. Pass list to Biz Dev Agent for outreach drafting
