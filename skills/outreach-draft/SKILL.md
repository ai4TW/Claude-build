# Outreach Draft

Write a cold email to a real estate agent prospect that gets a reply.

## The angle that works

Real estate agents lose deals to missed calls. Every unanswered call during a showing, open house, or client meeting could be a $10,000–$30,000 commission walking out the door. AllTheCalls solves this for $149/month — less than a single lost deal.

Don't lead with features. Lead with the problem they already feel.

## Email structure

**Subject line** — specific, curious, not salesy
- Good: "Sarah — quick question about missed calls"
- Good: "Your calls while you're at showings"
- Bad: "Revolutionize your real estate business with AI"

**Opening** — one sentence, reference something specific about them
- Their brokerage, city, a recent listing, their specialty (luxury, new construction, etc.)
- Shows you're not blasting a template

**The problem** — 2–3 sentences, paint the scenario
- They're at a showing. Their phone rings. They can't answer. That caller just called the next agent on the list.

**The offer** — 2 sentences max
- AllTheCalls gives them a 24/7 AI receptionist that answers every call in their name, qualifies leads, and books showings. $149/month, 5-minute setup, no contract.

**CTA** — one question, easy to say yes to
- "Want me to show you what it sounds like when a caller reaches your AI? Takes 30 seconds."
- Never ask for a meeting or a call in the first email.

## Personalization checklist

Before sending, confirm the email includes:
- [ ] Their actual first name
- [ ] Their brokerage name or city
- [ ] One specific detail (a listing, their specialty, their market)
- [ ] No generic phrases ("I hope this finds you well", "touch base", "circle back")

## Output

Save each draft to `shared-workspace/sales/outreach/[prospect-name].md`.

For batch outreach (10+ prospects), save as a CSV-ready format with columns: `first_name, email, subject, body`.
