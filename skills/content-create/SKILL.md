# Content Create

Produce this week's marketing content batch for AllTheCalls.

## Audience

Real estate agents. They are:
- Busy and mobile — reading on their phone between showings
- Skeptical of tech tools — they've been burned before
- Motivated by money — a missed call = a lost commission
- Persuaded by peers — they trust other agents more than vendors

Write like you understand their world. Reference showings, open houses, buyer calls, listing appointments. Never use words like "leverage", "synergy", "seamlessly", or "game-changer".

## Weekly content batch

### 3 LinkedIn posts

Mix these formats:

**Story post** — tell a specific scenario, make it vivid
> "An agent in Dallas was at a listing appointment last Tuesday. Her phone rang twice while she was with the sellers. She didn't answer — you don't pull out your phone mid-appointment. Both callers went to voicemail. Neither one left a message. One of them listed with another agent two days later."

**Educational post** — explain something practical
> How does an AI receptionist actually qualify a real estate lead? Here's the exact script it uses when someone calls asking about a listing...

**Results post** — social proof, real numbers
> One of our clients in Phoenix handled 47 calls last week without answering a single one herself. Here's what the AI captured for her...

### 2 Facebook group posts

For real estate agent communities. Conversational, start a discussion, don't pitch.
- "Quick question for the group — how do you handle calls when you're in a showing?"
- "Anyone else notice that most buyer leads call once and never leave a voicemail?"

### 1 email

One focused point. 150 words max. One CTA. Options:
- A scenario-based story leading to "this is why we built AllTheCalls"
- A tip for handling high call volume
- A client result (with permission)

## Output

- LinkedIn posts: `shared-workspace/content/linkedin/[date]-posts.md`
- Facebook posts: `shared-workspace/content/facebook/[date]-posts.md`
- Email: `shared-workspace/content/emails/[date]-email.md`

Flag anything needing human review (client names, specific stats) in `shared-workspace/content/needs-review.md`.
