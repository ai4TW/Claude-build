**Goal:** Re-engage, remove objections, and drive back to trial or purchase.
**Sequence:** 5 emails over ~21 days
**Tone:** Direct, empathetic, not pushy — treats them as a peer professional

## Sequence Summary

| # | Day | Subject | Goal |
|---|-----|---------|------|
| 1 | 3 | Still losing calls? | Re-engage, lower friction |
| 2 | 7 | This happened to an agent... | Amplify pain, create urgency |
| 3 | 12 | The two things that stop agents | Remove top objections |
| 4 | 17 | What [Agent Name] did in 30 days | Social proof, concrete ROI |
| 5 | 21 | Last email (and a genuine offer) | Final push, graceful exit |

**Technical notes:**
- Use first-name personalization on all emails
- Segment out anyone who converts at any point (stop the sequence)
- A/B test subjects on Email 2 and 4 (highest open rate impact)
- Plain text format outperforms HTML for cold lead re-engagement sequences
