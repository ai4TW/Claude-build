## Post 2 — Educational Post

People ask: "What does an AI receptionist actually say when it answers my calls?"

Here's the real script — what happens when someone calls an AllTheCalls client asking about a listing:

**AI answers:** "Hi, thanks for calling [Agent Name]'s office. I'm her assistant. She's with a client right now — can I help you?"

**Caller:** "Yeah, I saw the house on Maple Street. Is it still available?"

**AI:** "It is! Are you currently working with a buyer's agent, or would you be looking to work directly with [Agent Name]?"

**Caller:** "We don't have an agent."

**AI:** "Perfect. Can I get your name and the best number to reach you? She'll call you back within the hour. Also — are you pre-approved, or still in the early stages?"

That last question matters. It tells the agent whether they're getting a warm lead or a tire-kicker before they even call back.

The AI captures the name, number, property interest, and buyer status — then texts it to the agent immediately.

No voicemail. No "press 1 to leave a message." Just a qualified lead waiting in their SMS.
