## Post 2 — Observation / Tip

Anyone else notice this pattern:

Buyer calls about a listing. You miss it. They don't leave a voicemail. You see the missed call, call back 20 minutes later, and they don't answer.

That's it. That's the whole interaction. They've already moved on.

I read somewhere that over 80% of callers who get voicemail don't leave a message — and most of them don't answer when you call back either.

The window is basically: answer now, or lose them.

Tip that's helped me: even a text reply within 5 minutes of a missed call dramatically improves the callback rate. Something as simple as "Hey, I just missed your call — are you reaching out about a listing?"

It buys you time and keeps the conversation open.
