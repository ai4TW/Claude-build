## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Total runtime:** ~60 seconds
- **Music:** Upbeat but not distracting — corporate/motivational background track, fade down during key stats
- **VO talent:** Male or female, mid-30s energy, authoritative but approachable
- **Text overlays:** Key stats should appear as large, bold on-screen text (not just spoken)
- **CTA card (final 5 seconds):** Show URL prominently + "No credit card required"
- **Subtitles:** Include closed captions for accessibility and silent autoplay environments
