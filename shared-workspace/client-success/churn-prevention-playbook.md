# All The Calls — Churn Prevention Playbook

> **Primary trigger:** Client has 0 calls in the last 7 days
> **Goal:** Re-engage at-risk clients before they cancel

---

## Risk Tiers

| Tier | Signal | Action | Owner |
|------|--------|--------|-------|
| **Yellow** | 0 calls in 7 days | Automated check-in email | System |
| **Orange** | 0 calls in 14 days OR support ticket unresolved | CS rep personal outreach | CS Rep |
| **Red** | 0 calls in 21 days OR cancellation intent detected | CS rep phone call | CS Rep |
| **Critical** | Cancellation request submitted | Save call within 24 hrs | CS Lead |

---

## Tier 1 — Yellow Alert (0 calls, 7 days)

### Automated Email

**Subject:** Quick check-in — is your call forwarding set up correctly?

---

Hi [First Name],

I noticed your AI receptionist hasn't received any calls in the past week — which usually means one of two things:

1. **Call forwarding isn't active** — this is the most common fix (takes 2 minutes)
2. **You've been in a slow period** — totally normal, especially in [current season]

Either way, I want to make sure you're set up to capture every lead when calls pick back up.

**Quick fix:** Log into your dashboard and check that your forwarding number is active → [Check Forwarding Settings]

If you want me to verify your setup personally, just reply and I'll take a look right now.

[Your Name]
Client Success, All The Calls

---

### CS Rep Action
- [ ] Review client's Trillet dashboard to confirm if forwarding is broken or volume is genuinely low
- [ ] Add internal note to client profile: "Yellow alert — zero calls 7 days"
- [ ] If forwarding is broken: reach out proactively with fix before they notice

## Tier 2 — Orange Alert (0 calls, 14 days OR open support ticket)

### CS Rep Personal Email

---

Or if you'd rather I just walk you through it via email, reply and I'll send you the step-by-step.

[CS Rep Name]
Client Success, All The Calls
[Direct phone number]

---

### CS Rep Action
- [ ] Attempt email outreach (template above)
- [ ] If no response in 48 hours: try SMS or phone call
- [ ] Pull client's original onboarding checklist — identify any incomplete steps
- [ ] Review account for technical issues (forwarding error, AI configuration gaps)
- [ ] Document in CRM: "Orange alert — outreach attempted [date]"

---

## Tier 3 — Red Alert (0 calls, 21 days OR cancellation intent)

### CS Rep Phone Call Script

**Opening:**
> "Hi [First Name], this is [Name] from All The Calls. I'm calling because I want to make sure your AI receptionist is working for you — I noticed you haven't had any calls come through in a few weeks and I wanted to check in personally."

**Discovery questions:**
1. "Has anything changed with how you're handling incoming calls?"
2. "Have you forwarded calls to a different number recently?"
3. "Is there anything about the setup that isn't working the way you expected?"
4. "How are things going in your market right now?"

**If technical issue:**
> "I can fix that right now while I have you on the phone — it'll take less than 5 minutes. Can you log into your dashboard?"

**If seasonal slow period:**
> "That makes sense. A lot of agents go through quiet stretches. What I'd suggest is keeping the AI active so you don't miss the calls that do come in — even one lead this month could pay for the next year of service."

**If product disappointment:**
> "I really appreciate you sharing that with me. Can you tell me more about what you were expecting? I want to make sure we get this right for you — and if there's something we need to fix on our end, I want to know about it."

**If considering cancellation:**
> "Before you cancel, can I ask — is this about the cost, the results, or something technical? I want to make sure we've exhausted every option. I'd hate for you to leave and then miss a $15,000 commission next month because a call went to voicemail."

---

### CS Rep Action
- [ ] Attempt phone call (leave voicemail if no answer)
- [ ] Follow up voicemail with personal email
- [ ] Offer a free 30-minute setup review call
- [ ] Escalate to CS Lead if no response within 72 hours
- [ ] Consider offering 1-month credit to re-engage (requires CS Lead approval)

---

## Tier 4 — Critical (Cancellation Request Submitted)

### Save Call Protocol

**Response time:** Within 24 hours (same business day if possible)

**Who handles:** CS Lead (not frontline rep)

**Save call goals:**
1. Understand the real reason they're leaving
2. Resolve the underlying issue if fixable
3. Offer a retention incentive if warranted
4. If they still cancel: gather feedback and leave the door open

**Save offer menu** (offer in order, escalate as needed):

| Offer | When to Use |
|-------|-------------|
| Free setup review + fixes | Technical issue suspected |
| 1 free month | Cost concern, good history |
| Downgrade to Starter (if on Pro/Team) | "Too expensive" |
| Pause for 30 days | "Slow season" or temporary situation |
| 50% off for 3 months | High-value client, strong save case |

**Never offer:** Full refund without CS Lead + founder approval.

---

### Post-Cancellation (if save fails)

- [ ] Send exit survey (1 question: "What was the main reason you cancelled?")
- [ ] Log cancellation reason in CRM
- [ ] Tag for re-engagement campaign in 60 days ("Win-back" sequence)
- [ ] Send offboarding email with instructions to disable call forwarding

---

## Weekly Churn Review (CS Team)

Every Monday, CS team reviews:

1. **New Yellow alerts** (0 calls in 7 days) — automated emails sent?
2. **Pending Orange cases** — personal outreach completed?
3. **Active Red cases** — phone calls logged?
4. **Cancellation requests** — save calls scheduled?
5. **Closed saves** — what worked? Document for playbook updates

**Metric to track:** Monthly churn rate (target: < 3%)

---

## Common Reasons for Zero Calls + Quick Fixes

| Reason | Fix |
|--------|-----|
| Call forwarding disabled or broken | Re-enable forwarding in phone carrier settings |
| Wrong forwarding number entered | Update in All The Calls dashboard |
| Agent changed phone numbers | Update business number in account settings |
| Agent on vacation / inactive | Verify — no action needed, note in CRM |
| Genuine market slowdown | Reassure, keep active, note seasonal pattern |
| Agent using personal phone exclusively | Educate on forwarding setup |
| AI configuration error (greeting failing) | CS rep diagnoses via Trillet dashboard |
