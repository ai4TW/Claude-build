> **Use for:** Upgrade conversations via email, phone, or dashboard prompt
> **Best timing:** Day 7 check-in, after first busy period, or when call volume increases

## Script 2: Starter → Pro (Phone / Live Conversation)

**Opening:**
> "Hi [First Name], I was looking at your account and noticed you've been getting great call volume — [X] calls last month. That's fantastic. I wanted to reach out because I think you might be leaving some money on the table with your current plan."

**Discovery:**
> "Can I ask — when a caller leaves their number, are you following up with them by text as well as phone? ...And when leads come in, are they going directly into [their CRM]?"

**If they say no to either:**
> "That's exactly what I wanted to talk to you about. On Pro, we automatically text every caller within 2 minutes of the call — before they've even had a chance to call another agent. And we push the full lead profile directly into [CRM], so you never have to manually enter a lead again."

**Value anchor:**
> "At $249/month, you're spending about $8 a day. If SMS follow-up helps you close even one more deal this year, that's a 50x return. Does that math make sense for your business?"

**Close:**
> "I can upgrade your account right now — it takes about 60 seconds and your plan switches immediately. Want me to do that while I have you?"

**If they hesitate:**
> "Totally fair — no pressure. What I'll do is send you a comparison of what you're getting now versus what Pro includes, and you can decide when it feels right. Is email the best place to send that?"

## Script 4: Pro → Team (Phone / Live Conversation)

**Opening:**
> "Hi [First Name], I'm calling because I wanted to talk to you about something I think could really help your team."

**Discovery:**
> "How many agents are you currently working with, or do you have any referral partners who might benefit from having their own AI receptionist?"

> "Think about it this way: if one of your buyer's agents misses a call right now, that lead goes nowhere. On Team, that call still gets answered professionally, qualified, and logged — and you can see it. It's like having a receptionist desk for your whole office."

**Price framing:**
> "Team is $399/month. If you had [X] agents each on Starter, you'd be paying [X × $149]. Team is actually cheaper, and you get more features."

**Close:**
> "Should I set up a quick demo so you can see the team dashboard? It takes about 10 minutes and I think you'll see right away why agents love it."

## Objection Handling

### "It's too expensive."
> "I hear you. Let me put it in perspective: at [current plan price], you're spending about [$X/day]. If your AI helps you book one extra showing this month that leads to a closing, that's a $15,000 commission from an $8/day tool. The upgrade adds [key feature] — which most agents tell me pays for itself within a week. Would it help if I could show you the ROI based on your actual call volume?"

### "I'm happy with what I have."
> "That's great to hear — and it tells me the core product is working for you. I'm not trying to change something that's working. I just wanted to make sure you knew about [specific feature] because based on your usage, it looks like it could save you [time/money]. If it's not relevant right now, no worries at all — just wanted you to have the information."

### "Let me think about it."
> "Absolutely, take your time. Can I send you a side-by-side comparison so you have something to reference? And if you have questions when you're reviewing it, my direct email is [email] — I'll respond same day."

### "I need to talk to my partner / broker."
> "Of course. I can send you a one-page summary that outlines the plan features, pricing, and ROI data — something you can share easily. Would that be helpful?"
