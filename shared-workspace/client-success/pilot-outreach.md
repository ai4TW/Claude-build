## Tips for Finding Prospects

- Search Instagram: `#realestate[city]`, `#[city]realtor`, `#realtorlife`
- Search Facebook Groups: local real estate agent groups, city investor groups
- Look for agents posting listings, market updates, or open house promos
- Target solo agents and small teams (more likely to answer their own phones)
