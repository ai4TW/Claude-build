# RealtyVoice AI — Frequently Asked Questions

> **Top 10 questions from real estate agents**
> Use this document for onboarding, support, and website FAQ section.

---

## 1. Will my clients know they're talking to an AI?

**Short answer:** Only if you want them to.

Your AI receptionist answers in your name and brand: *"Thank you for calling Sarah Johnson with Compass — how can I help you today?"* Most callers don't know (or care) that it's AI, because the experience feels professional and helpful.

If you prefer full transparency, we can configure the AI to say "Hi, this is Jordan, an AI assistant for Sarah Johnson." Either approach works — agents in luxury markets often prefer transparent disclosure.

---

## 2. What happens if the AI can't answer a question?

The AI is trained to handle call qualification, scheduling, and FAQ responses. If a caller asks something outside its scope (e.g., "What's the exact price of 123 Main Street?"), it gracefully says: *"That's a great question — let me have [Your Name] follow up with you directly. Can I get your best number?"*

It never makes up answers or provides inaccurate information. When in doubt, it captures the lead and hands it to you.

---

## 3. How long does setup take?

**Most agents are live in under 10 minutes.**

1. Create your account (2 min)
2. Add your business details and call forwarding number (3 min)
3. Forward your calls to your RealtyVoice number (2 min)
4. Customize your greeting (3 min)

Our onboarding team can also set everything up for you on a 15-minute Zoom call if you prefer.

---

## 4. What if I'm already in a showing or a meeting?

That's exactly what RealtyVoice AI is for. Every call that comes in while you're unavailable gets answered professionally, the lead gets qualified, and all details are logged in your dashboard. You'll get an email summary so you can follow up the moment you're free.

You never miss a lead, even when you're face-to-face with a client.

---

## 5. Does it integrate with my CRM?

Yes. RealtyVoice AI integrates with:

- **Follow Up Boss**
- **KvCORE**
- **HubSpot**
- **GoHighLevel (GHL)**
- **Google Contacts** (basic)

Leads captured during calls are automatically pushed to your CRM with name, phone number, call notes, and qualification data. No manual entry required.

If you use a different CRM, contact us — we add integrations regularly.

---

## 6. Can I listen to call recordings?

Yes. Every call is recorded and stored in your dashboard. You can:

- Play back any call recording
- Read the AI-generated call transcript
- See the lead qualification summary
- Export call logs for your records

Recordings are stored for 90 days on Starter and Pro plans, and indefinitely on Team.

---

## 7. What happens after hours or on weekends?

Your AI receptionist never sleeps. It handles calls 24/7/365 — including evenings, weekends, and holidays.

You can configure after-hours behavior:
- **Qualify and log only** — AI captures leads, no interruptions to you
- **Urgent routing** — AI asks "Is this urgent?" and forwards to your cell if yes
- **Full availability** — AI handles everything and you review in the morning

Most agents set urgent routing on weekdays and qualify-only on weekends.

---

## 8. What if I want to change my script or greeting?

You can update your AI's script, greeting, tone, and qualification questions anytime from your dashboard — no coding required.

Changes go live within 60 seconds. We recommend reviewing your setup after the first week once you've seen what callers typically ask.

---

## 9. Can I cancel or pause my subscription?

Yes. There are no long-term contracts.

- **Cancel anytime** — No cancellation fees. Your AI stays active through your current billing period.
- **Pause** — Need a break? You can pause your subscription for up to 30 days (Pro and Team plans).
- **Downgrade** — Move to a lower plan at any time. Changes take effect at the next billing cycle.

We'll always try to work with you before you cancel — reply to any of our emails and we'll find a solution.

## 10. How is RealtyVoice AI different from a virtual assistant service?

| | RealtyVoice AI | Human VA |
|---|---|---|
| Available 24/7 | Yes | Rarely |
| Cost | $149–$399/mo | $1,500–$5,000/mo |
| Scales with call volume | Yes (unlimited) | No (billed per hour) |
| Response time | Instant | Minutes to hours |
| CRM integration | Automatic | Manual |
| Consistent quality | Every call | Varies by person |

Human VAs have a place — but for call answering and lead qualification, AI handles it faster, cheaper, and more consistently. Most agents use RealtyVoice AI for first-touch calls and their VA for complex follow-up tasks.
