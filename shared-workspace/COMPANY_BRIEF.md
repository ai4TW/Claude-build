# RealtyVoice AI — Company Brief

## What We Are
A white-label AI receptionist reselling business exclusively serving real estate agents.
We resell Trillet AI under the RealtyVoice AI brand.

## The Problem We Solve
Real estate agents miss calls constantly — showings, open houses, client meetings.
A missed call = a missed lead. The average commission is $10,000-$30,000.
No agent can afford to miss calls. But hiring a human receptionist costs $3,000-$5,000/month.
We give them a 24/7 AI receptionist for $149-$399/month.

## Our Business Model
| Tier | Our Price | Trillet Cost | Our Margin |
|------|-----------|--------------|------------|
| Starter | $149/mo | ~$49/mo | ~$100/mo |
| Pro | $249/mo | ~$99/mo | ~$150/mo |
| Team | $399/mo | ~$199/mo | ~$200/mo |

Target: 100 clients = $15,000-$20,000 MRR

## What the AI Receptionist Does
- Answers every call 24/7 in agent's name ("Thank you for calling Sarah Johnson with Compass...")
- Qualifies the lead (buyer/seller, timeline, pre-approved?)
- Books showings directly to agent's calendar
- Syncs to CRM (Follow Up Boss, KvCORE, HubSpot, GHL)
- Sends SMS follow-ups after every call
- Handles after-hours and overflow calls

## Setup Process (5 minutes per client)
1. Agent signs up on our portal
2. We create their Trillet sub-account via API
3. AI is trained from their website URL (automatic)
4. We generate a custom greeting with their name + brokerage
5. They forward their business line to our number
6. Done — AI is live
