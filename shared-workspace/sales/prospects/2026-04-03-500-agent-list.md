## How to Use This List

1. **Find contact info:** Search agent name on FastExpert.com, Zillow, or Google. Most have public profile pages with contact forms or phone numbers.
2. **Email lookup:** Try `firstname@brokerage.com`, `firstnamelastname@gmail.com`, or use Hunter.io on their domain.
3. **LinkedIn:** Search name + city for direct message outreach.
4. **Prioritize:** Solo agents with own-brand brokerage (own website name) = no reception support. eXp, Real Broker = virtual brokerages = no central office = prime targets.
5. **Qualification signal:** Personal Gmail address = no team admin. Own brokerage LLC = small operation. 5–25 sales/year solo = high call volume, limited bandwidth.

## Fit Scoring Guide
- ★★★★★ Solo, own brand/LLC, personal contact found, high activity
- ★★★★ Solo or small team, active market, likely no dedicated reception
- ★★★ Team-based but may need overflow/after-hours coverage
- ★★ Larger team — reception likely exists, but worth trying

## OUTREACH NOTES

### Highest-priority segments (sort your list by these):
1. **eXp Realty agents** — Virtual brokerage means zero central office reception. Every agent is on their own for calls.
2. **Real Broker / LPT Realty agents** — Same as eXp. 100% solo for call handling.
3. **Own-brand LLC/firm** — "Jane Smith Real Estate LLC" = Jane is answering her own phone (or missing calls).
4. **Luxury market agents** — A single missed call = $15K–$50K+ commission lost. ROI argument is compelling.
5. **7–15 year agents** — Established enough to have volume, not yet big enough to have staff.

### Finding contact info for these agents:
1. **Google: "[Name] realtor [city]"** → Usually surfaces personal website or Zillow profile with phone
2. **FastExpert profile** → fastexpert.com/top-real-estate-agents/[city-state]/ → click name → contact info
3. **Zillow agent search** → zillow.com/professionals/real-estate-agent-reviews/[city]-[state]/
4. **LinkedIn** → Most have profiles; direct message works well for warmer outreach
5. **State license lookup** (free, public):
   - Texas: license.trec.texas.gov
   - Florida: myfloridalicense.com
   - Arizona: azre.gov/licensee-lookup
   - Georgia: verify.sos.ga.gov
   - North Carolina: ncrec.gov/licensee-lookup
